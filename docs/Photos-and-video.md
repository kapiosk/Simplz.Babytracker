# Photos and video

Mostly for a stool worth showing a doctor, which is a thing that comes up more than anyone expects.

## Attaching one

Open an entry with the pencil, and at the bottom of the editor is **Photos and clips**. Pick one or
more from the phone — camera roll or straight from the camera.

An entry has to exist before anything can attach to it, so a brand new one is saved first and then
reopened. The usual order — log the poop, then photograph it — is unaffected.

**Limits:** 25 MB a photo, 200 MB a clip. Nothing is resized or re-encoded, so what comes off the
phone is what is stored. Anything larger, or anything that is not an image or a video, is refused
with a message saying so.

## Seeing them

Attachments appear as thumbnails under the entry in the log on **Track** and in the list on
**Report**. Tap one to open it full size; video plays and can be scrubbed.

**The read-only password can see them and cannot change them.** That is deliberate — a doctor being
able to look at the photograph is most of the reason for taking one.

## Removing them

The ✕ on a thumbnail in the editor removes that one. Deleting an entry deletes its photos with it.
Both are immediate and cannot be undone.

## Where they live

Files sit next to the database on the server, not inside it, so a copy of the data folder is the
whole backup. They are served only to someone who has signed in — they are not on a public URL that
could be guessed or shared by accident.

If the app is reachable from outside your home, put it behind HTTPS before there are photographs of
a child on it.
