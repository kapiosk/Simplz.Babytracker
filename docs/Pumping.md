# Pumping

Everything to do with pumping is on the **Pump** tab, and nothing to do with pumping is anywhere
else. It is about you rather than about the baby, which is why it has its own screen and stays out
of the baby's log, the chart, the report and the trends.

## A session

**Start pumping** works the way the feed and sleep timers do: tap once to start, tap again to stop.
The timer lives on the server, so locking the phone or picking up the other one makes no difference
to it.

When you stop, it asks how much came out. Tap a preset or type a figure. If you do not know yet —
the bottle is still on the counter, or somebody needs a hand — tap **Skip** and fill it in later
from the log; the session is still recorded, just without an amount.

Pumping does **not** end a running feed or sleep. The baby can perfectly well be asleep while you
pump, so all three can be running at once.

## What is in the fridge

The figure at the top of the screen is the stock, and it is not a number anybody has to maintain.
It works itself out from what has been logged:

- every pumping session **adds** what it produced
- every **breast-milk** bottle logged on Track **takes that amount out**
- formula does not touch it

So the figure moves when somebody gives a bottle on the other phone, without anybody opening this
screen. That is what the line at the bottom is reminding you of.

Underneath the figure is the arithmetic — where the count started, how much has gone in, how much
has come out — so the number can always explain itself rather than merely being asserted.

## Correcting it

Real fridges do not balance. Milk gets thrown away, a bottle gets made up and not logged, or you
find a bag in the freezer from a fortnight ago.

**Correct** (or **Set**, the first time) is for that: say what is actually there now. Everything
before that moment stops counting, and the tally starts again from your figure. It shows up in the
log as its own entry, so it is clear later why the count reads what it reads.

Before the first session is ever logged, the stock reads *nothing pumped yet* rather than a figure.
Breast-milk bottles from before then are not counted against it — they came out of a fridge this
count knows nothing about.

### If it goes negative

It will say so, in red, rather than quietly showing zero. A negative means more breast milk has
been given than this count ever saw going in, so something was missed — almost always a pumping
session that never got logged. **Correct** it and it starts again from the truth.

## The log

Every session and every correction is listed underneath, newest first, with how long it took and
what came out. Tap the pencil to change any of it, or to delete an entry outright.

**+ Add a session from earlier** is there for the ones you did not log at the time. It opens
pre-filled as a session that started twenty minutes ago and has just finished.

## The read-only password

The doctor password sees the stock, the arithmetic behind it and the whole log, and can change
none of it — the same as everywhere else in the app.
